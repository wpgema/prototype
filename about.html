Our story

A focused craft, built on consistency.

CV Bricket Sumatera is an Indonesian manufacturer and exporter specializing in premium coconut charcoal briquettes. We transform carefully selected coconut shells into products for international customers.

For us, dependable supply starts with attention at every stage: selecting raw materials, managing production, checking the finished briquettes, and preparing orders for shipment. We work to keep the process clear and responsive to each buyer's requirements.

Our aim is straightforward: deliver consistent products and build lasting working relationships, from Sumatra to global markets.

How we work

A considered process, from shell to shipment.

Each stage contributes to the shape, performance, and consistency of the finished briquette.

  1. 01

    Selection

    Coconut shells are selected and prepared as raw material.

  2. 02

    Carbonization & grinding

    Shells are carbonized, then charcoal is ground to controlled particle sizes.

  3. 03

    Mixing & pressing

    Prepared charcoal is mixed and pressed into the requested briquette shape.

  4. 04

    Drying & inspection

    Products are dried and checked before they are packed.

  5. 05

    Packaging

    Finished briquettes are prepared according to the agreed order requirements.
